Who am I?

Versão em português

Hello, my name is Kelvin Fellipe Da Silva De Souza. I was born in the city of Toledo, Paraná, on April 6, 2011. I am currently a student at IFPR Campus Toledo, which is actually an expansion of the IF campus in Assis Chateaubriand.

I have always been a good student; even though I have studied in public schools all my life, I am very dedicated to my studies and rarely fell below the passing grade. My education began at Escola André Zenere, in the city of Toledo itself. I stayed at that school from the early years until the fifth grade of elementary school and never had any major problems. Right after finishing the fifth grade, I had to change schools, since it only offered classes up to that level. I was transferred to Colégio Estadual Dario Vellozo, where I studied from the sixth to the ninth grade of elementary school. After that journey, I secured my place at IFPR.

Where I Live

Jardim Europa is a neighborhood located in the municipality of Toledo, in western Paraná. The neighborhood developed alongside the growth of the city and the expansion of residential areas. Over the years, new families have come to live in the region, contributing to the formation of the community and to the development of local businesses and services.

One curiosity is that the name “Jardim Europa” (Europe Garden) refers to the European continent, following a common practice in the creation of neighborhoods and subdivisions that are named after well-known places. The neighborhood is also part of the urban expansion process of Toledo, which has grown considerably since its colonization began in the 1940s. Today, Jardim Europa is an important residential area that is home to the daily lives of many of the city’s working residents.

TCTs: Health Care in the Jardim Europa Neighborhood (Toledo-PR)

1. Health Actions and Activities in Jardim Europa

In the Jardim Europa neighborhood, in Toledo, the promotion of health and quality of life takes place in community spaces and in the neighborhood's care facilities. Among the main local initiatives are:

  • Physical Activities at the Outdoor Gyms (ATI): Frequent use of the neighborhood squares by residents for stretching, walking, and exercising in the community's sports squares.
  • Care and Prevention at the Jardim Europa UBS (Basic Health Unit): Actions by the Basic Health Unit focused on vaccination, prenatal care, control of hypertension and diabetes, and preventive talks.
  • Community and Sports Projects: Encouragement of sports for young people and children on the neighborhood's courts and fields, preventing a sedentary lifestyle and promoting social integration.

3. Everyday Chronicle: "A Sunny Afternoon at Europa Square"

Theme: Health Care and Community Life in Jardim Europa

In the late afternoon, when the sun begins to set over the rooftops of Jardim Europa, the neighborhood square takes on a different life. It is the moment when the routine of work and study takes a short break for the care of body and mind.

Dona Maria, a longtime resident of Oxford Street, arrived slowly with her little dog, adjusting her steps on the walking track. Further ahead, near the equipment at the Senior Citizens' Gym, a group of young people chatted excitedly as they rested from an improvised soccer game on the nearby court.

— Today's walk was a good one, huh, Dona Maria? — one of the boys commented, catching his breath while drinking water from a small bottle.

— It sure was, my boy! We can't let the body stand still. Taking care of our health here in the neighborhood is what keeps the mind sharp and the heart strong to face everyday life — she replied with a friendly smile.

In that simple corner of Toledo, between a chat on the sidewalk and a walk in the evening breeze, Jardim Europa showed that a community's health is built on collective habit, friendship, and the appreciation of every shared space.
