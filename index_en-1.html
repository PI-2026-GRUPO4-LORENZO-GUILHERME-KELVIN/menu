Where We Live - Municipality of Toledo

The settlement of the municipality of Toledo began in the early 1940s, driven by the activities of Pinho e Terra Ltda. and later by Maripá, which divided the region into lots and attracted settlers from Rio Grande do Sul and Santa Catarina, most of them descendants of German and Italian immigrants. Toledo became politically independent on December 14, 1951, developing into an important center in western Paraná.


Located in the western region of Paraná, Toledo has gently rolling terrain and highly fertile red soil. Its humid subtropical climate supports its strong agricultural economy. The city is nationally recognized for its agribusiness production.

Contemporary Cross-Curricular Theme (TCT)

Macro-area: Health | Topic: Health Care

TCT Macro-area: Health

Central Topic: Integrative and Preventive Health Care

Target Audience: Middle and High School Students

Objective: To promote awareness of healthy habits, physical and mental well-being, and disease prevention.

1. Introduction

Health is not simply the absence of illness. It is a state of physical, mental, and social well-being. Studying health care helps students make conscious and independent decisions about their bodies and lifestyles.

2. Areas of Health Care

Physical Health

Regular physical activity, quality sleep, proper posture, and daily personal hygiene.

Healthy Eating

Balanced nutrient intake, reduced consumption of ultra-processed foods, and adequate daily hydration.

Mental and Emotional Health

Stress management, balance between studying and leisure, and seeking psychological support when necessary.

Disease Prevention

Updated vaccinations, regular medical appointments, and disease prevention.

3. Interdisciplinary Connection

Cross-curricular themes connect different school subjects:

  • Science / Biology: Understanding the human body, immune system, and nutrition.
  • Physical Education: Movement, ergonomics, and the benefits of sports.
  • Mathematics: Nutritional charts and public health statistics.
  • Language Studies: Critical reading of health information and medical misinformation.

4. Everyday Recommendations

  1. Drink enough water every day.
  2. Practice regular physical activity.
  3. Maintain a regular sleep schedule.
  4. Avoid excessive screen use before bedtime.
